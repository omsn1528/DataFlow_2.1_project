# DataFlow privacy policy (draft for Play Console)

DataFlow (developer: Always ON) measures your phone's mobile-data usage.

- The app has no internet permission and sends no data anywhere.
- Usage figures, history, settings and backups stay on your device.
- "Usage access", if you grant it, is used only to read Android's own data-usage counters
  (totals and per-app mobile data). The app does not read app content, messages or browsing.
- Notifications are used only for the usage alerts you configure.
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no accounts.
- Backup and CSV export are saved only to a file location you choose.
- You can delete all history in Settings > Clear history, or uninstall the app.
